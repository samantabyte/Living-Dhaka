# Known Limitations

- The district is an artistic compression, not a 1:1 reconstruction of Dhaka.
- Passenger boarding uses a lightweight silhouette animation rather than a cinematic character rig.
- Traffic uses simulation LOD and simplified collision at distance.
- Multiplayer is intended for private 3–4 player rooms on a trusted local or self-hosted server.
- Steamworks APIs require a Steamworks partner account and are not bundled.
- Tauri packaging requires Rust/Cargo, which was not installed at initial environment inspection.
